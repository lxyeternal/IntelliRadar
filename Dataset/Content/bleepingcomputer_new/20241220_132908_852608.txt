
	   
As the amount of remote workers continue to increase, video meetings have become a popular and useful way to collaborate with employees and coworkers all over the world. Working from home, though, can introduce unforeseen and embarrassing situations that occur in the background that you may not have wanted your co workers to see.

To protect against this type situation, Microsoft announced today at the Ignite 2018 conference that a feature called Background blur in Microsoft Teams is now generally available. This AI driven feature will use facial recognition to automatically blur out your background while in a video meeting using Teams.


Microsoft's AI driven Background Blur feature

This feature has already been been available in preview and today at the Ignite 2018 developer conference, Microsoft has announced that this feature is now generally available to all Microsoft Office 365 commercial customers.

To use background blur while in a video meeting, simply follow these steps:
