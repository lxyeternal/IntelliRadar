
	   
The ransomware keep on coming! Since the last article we have had 6 new ransomware infections released, but to weight it out, 6 new decryptors. Included in the list of new ransomware infections is a ransomware that is targeting Drupal web sites and encrypting their files.

By far, the bombshell this week was the announcement that TeslaCrypt was shut down and that the malware devs released the master decryption key.  As you can imagine this has made a lot of people very happy who were previously unwilling or unable to pay the ransom.

If I missed anything, please let me know and I will get it added to the article. A big thanks to all the people who have been hammering at ransomware on Twitter and elsewhere! This article is the combined effort of all of them.

May 14th 2016

New ransomware discovered called 8Lock8

A new ransomware was discovered by security researcher Daniel Gallagher that encrypts your data and then appends the .8lock8 extension to encrypted files. When it has finished encrypting the files it will display a READ_IT.txt ransom note that tells you to contact d1d81238@tuta.io or d1d81238@india.com to get payment information.

May 16th 2016

The Shade/Troldesh ransomware started using the .da_vinci_code extension for Encrypted Files

MalwareHunterTeam discovered that the Shade/Troldesh ransomware switched to using the .da_vinci_code extension when encrypting files. They also switched to using the email GraceseYoumans1983@gmail.com for payment instructions.

Decryptor released for the GhostCrypt Ransomware

A decryptor was released by security researcher Michael Gillespie for the GhostCrypt ransomware.  This ransomware calls itself CryptoLocker and encrypts a victim's data with AES encryption and then demands 2 bitcoins to get your data back. When it encrypts your files it will append the .Z81928819 extension,  and when finished, will create a ransom note called READ_THIS_FILE.txt  on your desktop.

A decryptor was able to be created so a victim can get their files back for free. More information can be found here: GhostCrypt (.Z81928819) Help & Support Topic - READ_THIS_FILE.txt.

New ransomware discovered called SNSLocker.

A new ransomware called SNSLocker has been discovered that is currently in development by a person named Saad and has not been released to the wild. This ransomware uses AES encryption and appends the .RSNSlocked extension to encrypted files.  It is currently targeting 229 extensions and is requesting $300 USD in bitcoins to purchase the decryption key. Security researcher bluesoul [dot] me, also found that their Command & Control server credentials were stored in the executable.


SNSLocker Ransom Screen

May 17th 2016

Emsisoft Releases decryptor for the Xorist Ransomware Family

Emsisoft released a decryptor for the Xorist Ransomware family. Though this ransomware has been out for quite a while, previously victim's needed to get help on an individual basis. Now that a formal decryptor has been made, anyone can decrypt their files on their own. More information about the decryptor can be found here: Emsisoft Releases Decryptors for the Xorist and 777 Ransomware.


Xorist Decryptor

The Xorist family of ransomware infections are created by a builder that allows a distributor to generate their own ransomware executable. More info about how the builder works can be found here: Emsisoft Releases Decryptors for the Xorist and 777 Ransomware.

New 777 Ransomware discovered and Emsisoft Releases a Decryptor

Malware analyst and developer of PortEx, Katja Hahn, discovered the new 777 ransomware. The 777 ransomware encrypts your data and then appends the  777 extension to encrypted files. It will also create a ransom note called

May 18th 2016

New ransomware called Zyklon Locker being Distributed

A new ransomware called Zyklon Locker was discovered by Katja Hahn and is actively being distributed. This ransomware appears to be a variant of the GNL Locker discovered last week. Like GNL, Zyklon Locker will append the .locked extension encrypte dfiles and will create the ransom notes UNLOCK_FILES_INSTRUCTIONS.html and UNLOCK_FILES_INSTRUCTIONS.txt. 

A support topic can be found here: Zyklon Locker (GNL) Support and Help Topic - .locked and UNLOCK_FILES_INSTRUCTIONS.html.

May 19th 2016

TeslaCrypt shuts down and releases Master Decryption Key

In a surprise ending, the developers of TeslaCrypt close shop and release the master decryption key. This key was then used by ESET to release a decryptor and for BloodDolly to update TeslaDecoder so that victims can get their files back for free.  More information can be found here: TeslaCrypt shuts down and Releases Master Decryption Key.


TeslaCrypt releasing Master Decryption Key

Website is Locked! Ransomware targeting web sites

A ransomware that targets and encrypts web sites was discovered by security researchers Benkow moʞuƎq and Double Blind. This ransomware has encrypted approximately 400 web sites and appears to be targeting a vulnerability in Drupal. Double Blind has stated that he has been tracking this ransomware since the middle of April.

This ransomware uses the 3M6SQh8Q6d2j1B4JRCe2ESRLHT4vTDbSM9 bitcoin address for every victim, but thankfully no one has paid as of yet.

May 20th 2016

Version 4.0 of the DMA Locker Ransomware was Discovered

Hasherezade discovered version 4.0 of the DMA Locker ransomware. When infected, your files will become encrypted, but the file names will remain the same.  In order to identify a DMA Locker 4.0 encrypted file, you can check the file in a hex editor and first 9 bytes will contain the strings !DMALOCK4. Even easier, you can upload an encrypted file to ID Ransomware and it can detect it.

When your files are encrypted it will create a ransom note called C:\ProgramData\cryptinfo.txt that contains a link to their payment site.  It will also display a lock screen as shown below and request 1.5 bitcoins to get your files back.
