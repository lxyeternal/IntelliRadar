
	   


The hacker known as TheHackerGiraffe has been promoting PewDiePie's YouTube channel through unwanted Internet-connected printer jobs and Chromecast hacks. In an audio Periscope recording, the hacker calls it quits after being told that the FBI may be building a case against him.

For about a month now, the hacker states he has been promoting PewDiePie's YouTube channel as a way to spread awareness regarding the lack of security in Internet-connected devices such as printers, Google Home, Smart TVs, and Chromecast devices. While some may consider what he is doing funny, and possibly even worthwhile, others do not find it is amusing at all and his activities will probably cause him problems with law enforcement.

In a Periscope live stream recording, the hacker explains that ever since he started sending print jobs to Internet-connected printers that promote PewDiePie's YouTube channel, and more recently streaming videos via Chromecast, he has been worried and anxious due to the constant stream of death threats and fear of law enforcement.
