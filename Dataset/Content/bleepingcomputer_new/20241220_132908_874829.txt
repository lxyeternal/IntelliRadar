
	   


A 14-year-old US teen has admitted to attempting to hack the Brussels Zaventem Airport on the day after brutal ISIS bombings had killed 32 people, on the morning of March 22, 2016.

Belgium authorities said the cyber-attacks took place on the night between March 22 and 23 but were unsuccessful.

According to a statement issued to Belgian press by the country's Federal Prosecution Office, the young hacker attempted to break into the airport's website and then escalate his access to core airport systems.

After being questioned by the FBI, the minor admitted his crimes. FBI investigators also executed a search warrant at the suspect's home.

Investigators said teen didn't act on any religious beliefs, and he only thought this would be a good time to hack the airport, as staff would be preoccupied with other tasks. Analysis of the teen's computer drives also didn't reveal any ties to ISIS or other terrorist groups.

The teenager didn't reveal what he intended to do after he would have gained access to the airport's website or internal network.

Because the suspect is a minor, authorities have not revealed his name, and also said they don't plan to disclose any information on the case for the time being.
