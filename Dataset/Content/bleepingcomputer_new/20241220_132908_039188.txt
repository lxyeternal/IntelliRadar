
	   
It has been discovered that the Radamant Ransomware Kit has been for sale on private exploit and malware sites since December 7th 2015.  This kit costs $1,000 to rent it for one month or potential buyers can test it for 48 hours for $100 USD. Included in this rental is a complete payment system, administrative console, statistics, the ability to modify extensions that will be encrypted, and technical support on getting the ransomware kit up and running. The only thing left for the buyer to do is distribute the ransomware using their own methods.

Below are some of the advertised features of the Radamant Ransomware Kit that were originally posted in Russian. We have used Google Translate to translate some snippets of the post into English:


Radamant Ransomware Kit
Good day, dear masters! 
Today I would like to introduce you to a new and flexible solution for converting your downloads. 
To begin, consider the technical aspects of the software: The client side

Written in C ++;
	Weight 70 kb;
	Encrypt all data carriers (HDD, USB-flash and Shared folder);
	Encrypting files masks to 1607 (with the possibility of real-time change) 
	AES-256 (32-character key for each file) with encryption key RSA-2048
	If you interrupt the encryption (shutdown / restart) starts encryption of a place to stay;
	When you restart the computer looks for new files for encryption;
	Working under a user account;
	Privilege escalation through social engineering (WMI);
	Removing the shadow copies of files and restore points Windows;
	Samoudalenie after decryption;

software is distributed only in this way and no more. 
It works for all countries. No restrictions.
