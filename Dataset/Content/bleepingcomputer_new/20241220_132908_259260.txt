
	   
As many of you know, unscrupulous tech support companies have been displaying online advertisements that try to scam visitors into thinking that their computer has a serious problem. This is done to trick the visitor into calling the listed support number so they can scare them into buying unnecessary services. Recently I have seen new scams that don't try to scare people but rather look like they are trying to help them.

From a marketing standpoint, scaring the visitor is a bad approach because the error messages that are displayed are not only confusing to most users, but also may cause them to panic and turn off their computer.


Fake BSOD Tech Support Scam

I mean, what person is going to look at the above screen and call the listed number?  The one that is freaking out who now thinks their computer is falling part? No way. Instead they are going to someone like me who will tell them that it is a big scam, while deep inside they may be wondering if there is actually something wrong.

To get past this panic and thus increase the the amount of callers, I have recently seen these scammers switch to advertisements that take a "we are here to help" approach rather than a 'let's scare the crap out them' one. These advertisements are ones I call interactive online tech support scams as they show a video of a woman calmly trying to help you, while asking questions and waiting for your response.

When visitors sees an advertisement like the one above they do not feel as threatened. Instead, I was told that they felt like their computer had a minor problem and that these people can help them with it. 

All kidding aside, this approach works. I know when I watched this the first time, I couldn't help watching the video and trying out each of the answers to the questions. When I showed it to my wife, who I bore all the time with this stuff, she didn't feel threatened, went through the whole video, and even though she knew it was a scam, she didn't feel as horrible about it. Unfortunately (or fortunately), not everyone knows someone like me and will feel safe with this advertisement while not realizing it's purely a scam. 

This is just one more trick up the scammer's sleeve to bilk you out of your hard earned money! Be careful out there!
