
	   
Wow, it has been a busy week for ransomware. This week we have a new offline ransomware called Bart from the makers of Locky, 5 other new ransomware infections, a new verison of Locky, and quite a few new decryptors.

Contributors and those who provided new ransomware info this week include: @demonslay335, @fwosar, @JakubKroustek, @PhishMe, @proofpoint, @JAMESWT_MHT, @siri_urz, @malwrhunterteam, @bartblaze, @DanielGallagher, @malwareforme, @nyxbone, @Seifreed, @trendmicro, @BleepinComputer. If you are interested in ransomware, I suggest you follow all of them on Twitter.

June 25th 2016

New offline Bart Ransomware, from the creators of Locky, being Distributed

PhishMe and ProofPoint both discovered a new ransomware being distributed by the actors behind the Locky Ransomware. This ransomware is an offline encryptor as it does not need to communicated with a Command & Control server to ransom your files. Instead of encrypting your files it will store them in a password protected zip archive and then demand 3 bitcoins in order to get the files back.

Malwarebytes security researcher S!Ri discovered a new ransomware called Satana that encrypts your data using a filecoder and then also installs a lockscreen on the computer's MBR, or Master Boot Record. This ransomware requests .5 bitcoins to get your files back. It is possible to remove the bootlocker by fixing the MBR, but the files will still remain encrypted.

Malwarebytes has also released a technical writeup for Satana and for those who wish to discuss this infection, you can post in our Satana Help and Support Topic.



June 26th 2016

The EduCrypt Ransomware tries to teach you a Lesson

A new ransomware, or should we call it EduWare, called EduCrypt was discovered by AVG security researcher Jakub Kroustek. This ansomware that tries to teach its victims a lesson about ransomware. Like other encrypting malware, EduCrypt will encrypt a victim's files, but instead of demanding a ransom, it gives the victim the password for free along with a reprimand. 



June 27th 2016

New Locky version adds the .Zepto Extension to Encrypted Files

A new version of the Locky Ransomware was released yesterday that uses a new naming scheme that appends the .zepto extension to encrypted files. With this new version, Locky will rename files to a name similar to 024BCD33-41D1-ACD3-3EEA-84083E322DFA.zepto.



June 28th 2016

Decrypted: MicroCop Ransomware demands 48 Bitcoins to get your files back

A new ransomware called MicroCopy was discovered by S!Ri and Trend Micro and analyzed by security researcher Mosh. This ransomware will encrypt a victim's files with DES encryption and then demand 48.48 bitcoins to get the files back. Yes, you heard me right. They want 32K USD for the decryption.

Sucks for ransomware devs, as Michael Gillespie was able to create a decryptor for the MicroCop ransomware so that victims can recover their files for free.  Instructions for using the decryptor can be found in the MicroCop Support and Help Topic.



Shade Ransomware starts using the Windows 10 Extension

Discovered by Fabian Wosar, of Emsisoft, a new variant of the Shade Ransomware was discovered that appends the .Windows10 extension to encrypted files.

June 29th 2016

AVG releases decryptors for numerous Ransomware Programs.

AVG released decryptors for the Apocalypse, BadBlock, Crypt888 (MirCop), Legion, SZFLocker, and TeslaCrypt ransomware infections.  Though there were already decryptors released for many of these infections, the decryptors for the Legion and SZFLocker infections are new.

June 30th 2016

The Unlock92 Ransomware targets Russian Speaking Victims

A new ransomware that targets Russian speaking victims was discovered. This ransomware will encrypt a victim's computer and then ask them to send a email to unlock92@india.com to get their files back.

Thankfully, Michael Gillespie was able to create a decryptor so that victims can get their files back for free. Information on this ransomware and instructions on how to download and use the decryptor can be found in our Unlock92 Ransomware Support and Help Topic.



New WildFire Locker Ransomware that may be a variant of Zyklon

A new ransomware called WildFire Locker was discovered that appears to be a copycat of the Zyklon Locker Ransomware. Unfortunately, at this time there is no way to decrypt files encrypted by this ransomware, but if you wish to discuss it, you can do so in our WildFire Locker Help and Suppot Topic.
