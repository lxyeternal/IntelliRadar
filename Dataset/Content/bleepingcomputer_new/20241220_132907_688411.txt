Office 365 is part of the Microsoft 365 software bundle that also comes with two additional offers, namely Windows 10 and EMS (short for Enterprise Mobility + Security).

The errors listed above may have occurred following the installation of any of the affected Office 365 client updates in Semi-annual and Monthly channels starting with version 1902 (build 11328.x).

Impacted Office 365 client updates

The Office 365 client updates that were impacted and subsequently removed from the Microsoft Update Catalog are listed below:

• Office 365 Client Update – Semi-annual Channel Version 1808 for x64 based Edition (Build 10730.20426)
• Office 365 Client Update – Semi-annual Channel Version 1808 for x86 based Edition (Build 10730.20426)
• Office 365 Client Update – Semi-annual Channel Version 1902 for x64 based Edition (Build 11328.20492)
• Office 365 Client Update – Semi-annual Channel Version 1902 for x86 based Edition (Build 11328.20492)
• Office 365 Client Update – Semi-annual Channel (Targeted) Version 1908 for x64 based Edition (Build 11929.20516)
• Office 365 Client Update – Semi-annual Channel (Targeted) Version 1908 for x86 based Edition (Build 11929.20516)
• Office 365 Client Update – Monthly Channel Version 1911 for x64 based Edition (Build 12228.20364)
• Office 365 Client Update – Monthly Channel Version 1911 for x86 based Edition (Build 12228.20364)
• Office 365 Client Update – Monthly Channel (Targeted) Version 1911 for x64 based Edition (Build 12228.20364)
• Office 365 Client Update – Monthly Channel (Targeted) Version 1911 for x86 based Edition (Build 12228.20364)

Microsoft adds that updated guidance on when these updates will be re-added to the Catalog will be posted within the same support article when available.

In related news, Redmond also released the December 2019 Patch Tuesday security updates on December 10 with security updates for 36 vulnerabilities.

Seven of them are classified as Critical and one zero-day privilege elevation vulnerability in Win32k actively exploited in the wild.

Intel also addressed 14 security vulnerabilities as part of December's Patch Tuesday, with seven of them being high and medium severity security flaws impacting multiple platforms including Windows and Linux.

Update: Microsoft says that the issue is now resolved and the affected updates are back in the Update Catalog again. More info on the Microsoft Update Catalog.
