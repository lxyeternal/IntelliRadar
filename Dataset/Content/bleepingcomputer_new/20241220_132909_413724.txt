
	   


Facebook announced that today it removed 265 Facebook and Instagram accounts, Facebook Pages, Groups and events for fake behavior originating from Israeli commercial entity Archimedes Group.

The company is a repeat offender of Facebook's multiple policies, including the one for misrepresentation, and engaged multiple times in coordinated inauthentic behavior.

Nathaniel Gleicher, Head of Cybersecurity Policy, says that Archimedes Group's activity focused on people in Nigeria, Senegal, Togo, Angola, Congo, Niger, and Tunisia, with some reach in Latin America and Southeast Asia.

This was done via fake accounts to run Pages and represented them "as locals, including local news organizations, and published allegedly leaked information about politicians."

Archimedes Group's influence was notable, with about 2.8 million accounts following one or more of its Pages and 5,500 accounts joining at least one of the groups it created.

Since December 2012, the offender spent about  $812,000 for ads on Facebook. The latest promotion was recorded in April 2019.

"We identified these accounts and Pages through our internal investigations into suspected coordinated inauthentic behavior. We have shared information about our analysis with industry partners and policymakers," says Gleicher.

Archimedes Group and all its known subsidiaries are now banned on Facebook and was served a cease and desist letter.
