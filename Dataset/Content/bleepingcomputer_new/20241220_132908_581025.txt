
	   


Mozilla Firefox will soon generate a "Protection Report" that shows a daily summary of the types and amounts of trackers that were blocked using the browser's Content Blocking feature.

With the release of Firefox 65, Mozilla introduced a redesigned Content Blocking feature that allows users to block tracking scripts and third party cookies. In May 2019, Firefox 67 was released that also included fingerprinting and cryptominer blocking as well.

In the current Firefox 69 Nightly builds, Mozilla has now introduced a mockup of a new "Protection Report" that lists the type and amount of tracking and unwanted scripts that were blocked over the past 7 days.

Firefox Tracking Protection Report

It should be noted that at this point, the Protection Report is simply a mockup and not actual data from your browser. The full working feature is still being developed and a hard target Firefox version has not been decided at this point.
