The new Office 365 ATP Campaign Views feature is available for customers with Office 365 Advanced Threat Protection Plan 2, Office 365 E5, Microsoft 365 E5 Security, and Microsoft 365 E5 plans.

"The additional context and visibility available in these campaign views provide the full story of how attackers targeted the organization and its users and how their defenses held up (or not)," Microsoft explains.

Provides an inside look in phishing campaigns

The new Office 365 ATP feature allows security professionals to quickly adapt an organization's defenses based on the infrastructure, as well as sender names and addresses used by attackers in email-based malicious campaigns, by helping them keep track of the various changes the operators make to evade attack defenses.

Campaign Views does this by collecting and providing extensive information about phishing campaigns, expanding the number of campaign indicators for easier blocking.

The information it provides includes but it's not limited to the timeline, the deployed attack infrastructure, and stats about malicious emails that reached the organization.

On the whole, with the help of Campaign Views, an organization's security team can quickly:

• See summary details about the campaign, including when the campaign started, the sending pattern and timeline, how big the campaign was and how many users fell prey to it.
• See the list of IP addresses and senders used to orchestrate the attack.
• Assess which messages were blocked, ZAPped, delivered to junk or quarantine, or allowed into the inbox.
• See all the URLs that were manifested in the attack
• Learn if there are users that have fallen prey to any attacks and clicked on the phishing URL.

The information gathered using Campaign Views makes it a lot easier to investigate, track, and remediate threats as detailed below:

• Remediate compromised/vulnerable users
• Improve security posture by eliminating configuration flaws seen
• Investigate related campaigns that use the same indicators of compromise
• Hunt and track threats that use the same indicators of compromise

"To thwart these well-orchestrated attacks, Office 365 Advanced Threat Protection leverages a multi-layered defense system that uses a combination of machine learning, advanced heuristics, rich detonation capabilities, targeted capabilities to spot business email compromise, and massive security intelligence sources that come together during mail-flow as well as after email delivery," Microsoft says.

When pairing Campaign Views "with powerful tools like Office 365 ATP Threat Explorer and Office 365 ATP Threat Trackers," the new feature "can help organizations comprehensively and effectively improve their security posture, quickly remediate issues, and drive more thorough investigation, hunting, and response steps to help secure the organization."


Office 365 ATP email protection (Image: Microsoft)

More Office 365 security-focused changes

Microsoft's development team is also working on adding recommended security profiles for Office 365 ATP and Exchange Online Protection (EOP) later this month.

In November, Redmond announced the release of Office 365 ATP enhanced compromise detection and response, a new feature designed to help detect breaches.

This new capability also enables Security Operations (SecOps) teams to remediate hacked accounts, and to automatically detect and investigate suspicious users.

The company also enabled Authenticated Received Chain (ARC) for all for Office 365 hosted mailboxes in October to check authentication results within Office 365 and improve anti-spoofing detection.
