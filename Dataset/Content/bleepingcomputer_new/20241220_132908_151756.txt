
	   


Adobe has published their monthly Patch Tuesday updates for the month of May 2019. These updates includes fixes for vulnerabilities in Adobe Bridge CC, Adobe Experience Manager, and Adobe Dreamweaver.

None of the vulnerabilities fixed are labeled as Critical, but there are numerous information disclosure vulnerabilities and a privilege escalation.

Adobe Security Updates Summary:

APSB19-37 Security update available for Adobe Bridge CC

Adobe released a security update for Adobe Bridge CC that fixes an out-of-bounds read bug that could lead to information disclosure.

This update fixes a privilege escalation vulnerability in Adobe Dreamweaver that is caused by "insecure library loading". This is most likely a DLL hijacking vulnerability.
