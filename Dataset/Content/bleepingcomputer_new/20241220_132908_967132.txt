
	   
Anonymous recently announced the public posting of names, phone numbers, email address, and passwords of over 1,400 UN officials.  Anonymous states that they retrieved this information by hacking into the United Nations Climate Change web site in protest of the over 200 arrests at the recent Global Climate March in Paris.



Anonymous claims that they could easily have defaced the site, but felt that it would not hurt them as much as the leaking of these account information. They further state that these "hacks are not just against Police brutality, these are also against Cop21 to support Global Climate March."

Whether the leaked data is authentic has not been verified at this time.
