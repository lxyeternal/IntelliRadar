
	   
Last week, the FBI released their IC3 2015 Internet Crime Report that provides a breakdown of various Internet related criminal activity that was reported to them over the year.  According to this report Business Email Compromise, Email Account Compromise, and Ransomware are the hot topics this year with huge losses shown in all three categories.

It should be noted that the statistics in this report are based on the U.S. victim's that actually reported the loss and activity to the FBI.  When you take into consideration that this type of criminal Internet activity is global, the scale of the attacks and losses generated is incredible.


2015 Overall Statistics

Business Email Compromise

Business Email Compromise, or BEC, is when business email accounts are compromised through phishing, impersonation, or other tactics. The scammers then use these accounts to make what appear to be legitimate requests from an executive or other manager to transfer money to accounts in other countries or provide private information. 

According to this report, business email compromise had a reported loss of almost 250 million dollars.

This type of criminal activity is similar to BEC, but the scammers target individuals or individual professionals instead of a business. For example, once an account is compromised they may contact all of the people on the person's contact list and request money be transferred due to an emergency investment or try to retrieve other sensitive information.

Ransomware

If you are a regular visitor to my site, then you known Ransomware has been on a rapid rise this year.  With usually at least one new ransomware released weekly, it is no surprise to find that the FBI also saw a dramatic increase of ransomware victims in 2015.  

In 2015 the IC3 reports that there were 2,453 reported ransomware victims and a total of 1.6 million dollars in losses. I am scared to see what 2016 will bring.
