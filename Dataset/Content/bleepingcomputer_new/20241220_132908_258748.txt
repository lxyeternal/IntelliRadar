On May 18th, the TeslaCrypt devs announced on their TOR site that the TeslaCrypt ransomware was closed, released the master decryption key, and then apologized as seen below.


Message from TeslaCrypt Devs on May 18th 2016

According to Mikko, as of a few hours ago a modified message appeared on the TOR TeslaCrypt site that now recommends BloodDolly's TeslaDecoder application to decrypt your files.


New TeslaCrypt TOR message as of 5/22/16

BloodDolly is a security researcher who has been actively following every release of TeslaCrypt. He has done a great service to all TeslaCrypt victims and has been able to help numerous victims decrypt their files for free in the past. Information on how to use his tool to decrypt TeslaCrypt can be found here:TeslaCrypt shuts down and Releases Master Decryption Key.
