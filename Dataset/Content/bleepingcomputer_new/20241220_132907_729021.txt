
	   


Mozilla announced the addition of persistent private browsing in the Firefox web browser for iOS which will allow users to stay in Private Browsing mode at all times, even between browsing sessions.

The private browsing feature in Firefox for iOS allows one to browse the Internet using an iPhone or iPad device without any web history or cookies being saved on the device. The only exception to the rule are new bookmarks which will be saved for later reference.

Also, it makes it very easy to quickly switch to normal browsing mode with the tap of a finger, automatically removing all tabs from the private browsing session once the other mode has been enabled.

It's important to say that, while browsing history will not be saved, Private Browsing mode does not make users anonymous while browsing the web since the websites they visit and their ISPs will still be able to track them.

The new Firefox for iOS 15.0 build adds an extra feature to the private browsing mode, preserving private tabs when exiting the application and allowing users to continue their browsing session from where they left off.

According to Mozilla, "Keeping your private browsing preferences seamless is just another way we’re making it simple and easy to give you back control of the privacy of your online experience."


Persistent private browsing Firefox for iOS

Extra options for settings, menu, and new tabs

The new Firefox for iOS release comes with a new menu and settings layout designed to more closely match the design available in the desktop version of the application.

"Now you can access bookmarks, history, Reading List and downloads in the “Library” menu item," says Mozilla's announcement.

Firefox for iOS 15.0 also features new organization options for the New Tabs feature, making it possible for users to "choose to have new tabs open with your bookmark list, in Firefox Home (with top sites and Pocket stories), with a list of recent history, a custom URL or in a blank page."


New Tab options

The last addition to the new build is the option to reorder tabs by drag-and-dropping them to the tab tray or the tab bar making it easier to customize "Firefox Home with top sites and Pocket content."
