
	   


Google has announced new privacy and security features that allow you to use incognito mode in more properties, monitor your saved passwords for compromises, and manage how your data is used on YouTube.

Earlier this year, Google added privacy controls that let you auto-delete your Web and App activity, made your privacy settings more accessible, and enabled incognito mode in various Google apps.

With today's announcement, Google is unveiling four new privacy features that will allow users to be more secure, have greater control over their data stored at Google, and have privacy features that are consistent across Google properties.

Configure YouTube Auto-Delete

Google has also integrated their Password Checkup tool into the Google Password Manager, which will warn users when their saved passwords are weak, reused too often, or have been compromised in a data breach.



Starting next week, users will be able to tell Google Assistant to delete their Assistant activity, whether it be what you just said or everything you asked last week.



Finally, Google added incognito mode to YouTube earlier this year and it snow available in Google Maps as well.

"When you turn on Incognito mode in Maps, your Maps activity on that device, like the places you search for, won’t be saved to your Google Account and won’t be used to personalize your Maps experience. You can easily turn on Incognito mode by selecting it from the menu that appears when you tap your profile photo, and you can turn it off at any time to return to a personalized experience with restaurant recommendations, information about your commute, and other features tailored to you. Incognito mode will start rolling out on Android this month, with iOS coming soon."
