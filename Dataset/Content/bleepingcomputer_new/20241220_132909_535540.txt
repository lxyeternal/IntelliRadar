Yesterday, we reported that BitDefender made a GandCrab decrypter available for versions 4 through 5.1. Soon after the tool was released, the ransomware developers released a new 5.2 version, which is no longer decryptable.


GandCrab Decrypter

While the GandCrab developers may be quick to react to new developments, there affiliates seem to be slower. This is because, at least one affiliate is continuing to distribute the older 5.1 version that is decryptable, rather than switching to the new 5.2 version, which is not.
