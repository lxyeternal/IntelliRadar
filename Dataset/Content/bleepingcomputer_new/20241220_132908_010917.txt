
	   


The cyber division of the Ukrainian police took to pieces an operation that made money by registering accounts used to send spam through various services, including email and social networks.

The bot farm allowed its cybercriminal customers to buy large numbers of active accounts for multiple online services. These were then used to deliver unsolicited messages.

Raids at addresses in six cities

This was possible with multi-SIM card modems, which were present in high numbers at the raided addresses. An investigation into the operations of the bot farm revealed that the people running the illegal business adapted to the needs of their clients.

Apart from email and social networks, it is alleged that the electronic equipment was used to signup to payment systems, too.

According to the police announcement on Tuesday, the illegal service offered accounts that required phone verification, which is typically used to prevent automatic registrations and for recovering control over the resource if access is lost due to hijacking or forgetting the login password.

As for the purpose of the accounts, authorities say that they were used for distributing scam messages (alleged car winnings), spam, and phishing.

Fake landmine reports

It appears that the bot farm was also used to spread false reports about areas cleared of explosive mines. The Eastern part of the country is still under Russian occupation and shots are fired every day.

People were displaced from the occupied zones and a contact line has been established to separate the government-controlled areas. Landmines are common near the contact line and efforts are being made to demine the area to ensure people safety.

Police officers and the Main Investigative Directorate of Ukraine's National Police carried out searches at houses, apartments, garages and rented offices where the group set up the illegal activity.



To anonymize the bot farm traffic, the operators ran connections through VPN services and the Tor network. Details of how the officers were able to discover the physical addresses remain undisclosed.



The equipment found during the raids was seized and will be examined for additional information that would help with the legal proceedings.

A pre-trial investigation has started and the perpetrators will be prosecuted for extortion, terrorist acts, and for deliberately releasing false reports that threatened citizen's security.
