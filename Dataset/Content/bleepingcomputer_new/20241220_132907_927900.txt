
	   


Computer security is such a serious business with the risk of financial harm, data loss, and hardware damage that it can be a nice change of pace to run into a scam that brings a chuckle. Such is the case with a phishing scam that pretends to be an email informing you that Google has awarded you $2.5 million as part of a giveaway for being a faithful user of Google's services.

Now that is not to say that people won't fall for a ludicrous scam like this and send the phishers their personal info, but maybe this article will help you spot it as a scam and give you a laugh at the same time.

Powered by Google

This scam comes in the form of an email with a subject line of "Powered by Google" that states "You have been selected a winner for using Google services.".

Powered by Google Scam Email

Attached to this email is an attachment named "Official Winning Letter by Google and mastercard visa 2019.pdf". This attachment, shown below, states that you won a Google Visa/MasterCard (GVMC) Award with a prize of $2,500,000.00 USD!

To claim your prize, all you have to do is fill out the claims form and send it back to them. Once the scammers receive it, they will follow up with more emails, which I assume will be to get your banking details, your social security number, and any other detail they scan squeeze out of you.


Scam Attachment (Click to Enlarge)

It goes without saying that any email stating you won money should be considered a scam and that you should not reply to the scammers or send them any information.
