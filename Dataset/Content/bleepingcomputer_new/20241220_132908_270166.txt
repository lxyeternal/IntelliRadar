
	   
Looks like even ransomware developers take time off for the holidays as there was not much activity over the past couple of weeks.  We have seen mostly new variants being release, with the biggest being CryptoMix.

Otherwise, just a few small in development ransomwares being released. Hopefully, this downward trend will continue, but if I could hazard a guess, we will see increased released towards the end of the month.

Contributors and those who provided new ransomware information and stories this week include: @demonslay335, @fwosar, @malwareforme, @campuscodi, @FourOctets, @struppigel, @LawrenceAbrams, @malwrhunterteam, @BleepinComputer, @Seifreed, @PolarToffee, @hexwaxwing, @DanielGallagher, @jorntvdw, and @siri_urz.

December 25th 2017

New Blind Ransomware variant

Michael Gillespie discovered a new variant of the Blind Ransomware uploaded to ID Ransomware that appends the .[].blind2 extension to encrypted files and drops a note named How_Decrypt_Files.txt.

December 28th 2017

Tastylock Cryptomix Ransomware Variant Released

Michael Gillespie discovered a new variant of the CryptoMix ransomware that appends the .tastylock extension to encrypted files and changes the contact emails used by the ransomware. 



New SamSam variant

Michael Gillespie discovered a new SamSam/Samas variant uploaded to ID Ransomware that appends the .weapologize extension and drops a note titled 0009-SORRY-FOR-FILES.html.

New SQ_ variant

Michael Gillespie discovered another variant of the "SQ_" ransomware uploaded to ID Ransomware with "BA_" prepended to filenames and a note named BA_ IN YOUR FILES..txt.

December 29th 2017

Pulpy Ransomware discovered

Siri discovered a new ransomware being dubbed Pulpy Ransomware.



MadBit Ransomware discovered

Siri discovered a new ransomware called MadBit that appends the .enc extension to encrypted files.



January 2nd 2018

Security Summary: In Development Heropoint Ransomware

This is a quick analysis of the in development infection called Heropoint Ransomware that was discovered by Lawrence Abrams. This article will contain technical information related to how it infects a computer, how it is distributed, and whether it can be decrypted.



Korean File-Locker Ransomware changes extension

Lawrence Abrams discovered a new variant of the Korean File-Locker ransomware that now uses the .razy extension.

MMM Ransomware Discovered

Michael Gillespie discovered the MMM Ransomware, which appends the .triple_m extension to encrypted files and saves the encrypted key in a corresponding *.info file. Another variant uses the .0x009d8a extension. It also drops a ransom note named RESTORE_triple_m__FILES.html. It may be decryptable.



In dev Google Crypt Ransomware

Lawrence Abrams found a new in development ransomware called Google Crypt. Does not do anything currently other than show the lock screen.



January 3rd 2018

Older Xorist Variant Discovered

Lawrence Abrams discovered an older ransomware that was identified by Michael Gillespie  as Xorist. This variant appends the .cryptedx and can stil be decrypted by Emsisoft's decryptor.



January 4th 2018

Server Cryptomix Ransomware Variant Released

The devs behind the Cryptomix ransomware just keep pushing them out. A new Cryptomix variant was released last week that appends the .SERVER extension to encrypted files and changes the contact emails used by the ransomware. 



New Turkish Globe2 variant

Michael Gillespie discovered a Turkish Globe Ransomware that uses the .vrmrkz extension. It is still decryptable by Emsisoft's decryptor.



 

New Blind Ransomware variant

Michael Gillespie discovered a new variant of the Blind Ransomware that appends the .[email].leon extension to encrypted files.




That's it for this week! Hope everyone has a nice weekend!
