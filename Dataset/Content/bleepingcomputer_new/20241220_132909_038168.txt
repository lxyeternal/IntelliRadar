A scam email campaign is underway that states you won a new car through a BMW Lottery. These scam emails will have subject lines similar to "Claim Your Car and Check With your Winning Code" as show shown below.


Fake BMW Lottery Email

The emails state that you won a brand new BMW and that you need to claim the prize by sending in your full name, address, and mobile number.  The full text of this scam email is shown below.

This information can then be used by the attackers to perform identity theft on the recipient or to gain access to their financial accounts.
