
	   


Whether it be malware devs contacting us about our stories or commenting in our forums, we all know that the ransomware developers monitor researchers and technology sites for information about their programs. Nothing shows this better, than a ransomware that recently decided to adopt the name given to it by researchers.

In July, researchers from CrowdStrike reported that two different ransomware infections were being operated under the same BitPaymer name.  After analyzing the ransomware, it was discovered that there were both code, ransom note, and payment portal similarities between the two ransomware variants.


Both ransomware variant using the BitPaymer name

This led CrowdStrike to believe that a threat actor from the INDRIK SPIDER cybercrime group, who created BitPaymer, splintered off and created their own ransomware variant.

Due to the similarities and confusion of both variants using the same name, CrowdStrike dubbed this new variant DoppelPaymer.

It appears that the the devs behind this new ransomware variant read CrowdStrike's report and decided to adopt the name DoppelPaymer for their ransomware as can be seen on their Tor payment site below.


New DoppelPaymer Payment Site
