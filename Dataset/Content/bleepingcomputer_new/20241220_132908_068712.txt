
	   
Being infected with Ransomware is scary enough, but there are certain ones that my kids and others helped me pick out that they think are downright creepy.  So as a little Halloween treat, I have put this article together to showcase what I, and others consider, the creepiest ransomware infections.

I didn't go into detail about each ransomware, but included links to relevant articles for those who wish to learn more about them.

I would like to wish everyone a Happy and safe Halloween!

Jigsaw Ransomware

The Jigsaw ransomware uses the iconic Billy the Puppet character from the popular Saw movies.  Though the puppet is creepy, what is worse is this thing actually deletes your encrypted files after a certain amount of time. Thankfully, Jigsaw is easily decrypted.

Nagini Ransomware

The Nagini Ransomware uses Voldemort from the Harry Potter series as the background for this ransomware. This ransomware is named after Voldemort's pet snake Nagini.


Nagini Ransomware

Onyx Ransomware

Though this ransomware doesn't look too creepy, the mask at the bottom of the ransom note may be recognizable from the Spirited Away anime movie.  Without giving away the plot, this spirit is not so nice :(
