
	   


Microsoft released the November 2019 Office security updates, bundling a total of 17 security updates and five cumulative updates for seven different products, 15 of them patching flaws allowing unauthorized access to information.

To download security updates for your Microsoft Office installations, you have to click on the corresponding Knowledge Base article in the table below and scroll down to the "How to download and install the update" section to download the update packages for each product.

Patched Microsoft Office vulnerabilities

Out of the 17 Office security updates released by Microsoft today, six of them patch information disclosure bugs (CVE-2019-1442, CVE-2019-1443, CVE-2019-1446, CVE-2019-1448, CVE-2019-1402, and CVE-2019-1446) within Office 2016, Excel 2016, Office 2013, Excel 2013, Office 2010, Excel 2010, SharePoint Server 2019, SharePoint Enterprise Server 2016, SharePoint Enterprise Server 2013, SharePoint Foundation 2013, SharePoint Foundation 2010, and SharePoint Server 2010.
