
	   


Personal information belonging to over half a million students going back the 2008-2009 school year, parents, and staff members of San Diego Unified School District (SDUSD) may have been compromised in a data breach incident.

An unauthorized person baited the staff with phishing emails to collect credentials to log into the district's network services.

Massive information trove

The data breach exposed personally identifiable details of student and selected staff, including names, dates of birth, mailing and home addresses, telephone numbers, social security numbers and/or state student ID numbers.

Additionally, the attacker could view data about the students' parents or guardians, and emergency contacts of the district's employees.

Another type of data exposed to the intruder includes staff benefits information (health benefits enrollment information, beneficiary identify information, dependent identity information, savings or flexible spending account information),

Staff payroll and compensation figures were also available to the attacker, who could view paychecks and pay advices, deduction information, tax information, direct deposit financial institution name, routing number and account number, salary and leave information.

Almost a year of unauthorized access

It is unclear if the attacker copied the information, but they definitely had access to it between January and November 2018.

According to the notification letter from SDUSD, the staff became aware of the intrusion in October and continued investigating without alerting the trespasser. Completing this stage of the investigation is also the reason in delaying the public disclosure.



The San Diego Unified Police and Information Technology staff discovered the attack following several reports of phishing emails received by staff members across the district.

"School police have identified a subject of the investigation and blocked all stolen credentials. We cannot say more due to the ongoing nature of the investigation," SDUSD communicates.

At the moment, all victims have been notified and the staff members whose accounts were compromised had their passwords reset. Extra security measures have been implemented to resist against similar attacks in the future, SDUSD says.
