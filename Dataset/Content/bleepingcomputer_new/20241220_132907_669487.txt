
	   


Microsoft is in the process of expanding the Office 365 Advanced Threat Protection (ATP) capabilities with attack flow overviews of malware attacks targeting organizations.

This new feature builds upon the Office 365 ATP Campaign Views feature that rolled out in public preview back in December 2019 and it provided security pros with inside info in phishing attacks starting their organizations.

Microsoft is working on including additional visibility and context into malware attacks targeting its customers' organizations, on top of the already available inside look at phishing campaigns.

Inside look within malware campaigns

This will allow security professionals to see the big picture behind a malware campaign by better understanding their scope and responding to them more efficiently.

"We're expanding the functionality of our popular Campaign Views feature beyond phish," Microsoft explains on the new feature's Microsft 365 roadmap entry.

"You'll now see malware campaigns as well. We're adding an interactive timeline, and developing a process for automated campaign write-ups.

"We're also working to surface Campaign Views in additional views, so that you can easily refer to them from wherever you are in the investigation process."


Example Office 365 ATP phishing campaign view (Image: Microsoft)

Roll out expected in June

Microsoft is planning to roll out this new Office 365 ATP capability expansion during June 2020, to make it generally available to all Office 365 environments with an ATP plan that comes with Campaign Views support.

The Office 365 ATP Campaign Views feature is available for customers with Office 365 Advanced Threat Protection Plan 2, Office 365 E5, Microsoft 365 E5 Security, and Microsoft 365 E5 plans. You can learn more details about campaign views in Office 365 on Microsoft's documentation website.

Redmond previously launched an enhanced breach detection capabilities for Office 365 ATP to help Security Operations (SecOps) teams to discover breaches easier, as well as to remediate hacked accounts and quickly find and investigate suspicious users.

Office 365 will also block harmful content regardless of custom tenant configs unless manually overridden and automatically check Microsoft Office documents against known threat profiles and risks before letting users open them.
