The StockX sneaker and streetwear resale site has started sending out emails to all of their users stating that they need to reset their passwords due to a system update. While these emails are legitimate, as they do not provide much details, users have been concerned that they are phishing attempts or their accounts are being hacked.

These emails have a subject of "Please reset your StockX password" and do not provide much info other than saying "We recently completed system updates on the StockX platform. To access your account, reset your password by clicking below."

Password Reset Email from StockX

After receiving these unexpected emails, many users, including friends and family, were concerned [1, 2, 3, 4] that they were being hacked or that the company had a breach. Furthermore, when they attempted to login to their accounts, users are told that a "Password Reset Required" before they can login.



In response, the StockX Twitter account has been rapidly replying to questions about these emails by stating that "The password reset email you received is legitimate, was sent to all users and came from our team."



Example of how not to send password reset emails

In today's paranoia caused by constant data breaches and endless streams of phishing emails, this email notification is a perfect example of how not to require users to reset their passwords.

If StockX had provided more detailed information and included a link to a quick update on their site, I would not have received a bunch of calls and their StockX support and Twitter accounts wouldn't be flooded with people who are concerned.

BleepingComputer has contacted StockX with questions about these emails and their system update, but have not heard back at this time.

Update August 03 12:46 EDT: StockX responded to BleepingComputer's inquiries saying that the password reset emails were sent after detecting suspicious activity and not because of a system update, as initially stated.


StockX was recently alerted to suspicious activity potentially involving our platform. Out of an abundance of caution, we implemented a security update and proactively asked our community to update their account passwords. We are continuing to investigate.
