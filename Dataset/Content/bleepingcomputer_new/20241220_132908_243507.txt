
	   


Tonight at 8 PM EST, Facebook will be kicking off their 2019 Capture the Flag event where security researchers, professionals, and enthusiasts compete to be the fastest to solve the most puzzles in order to win cash prizes and prestige.

For those not familiar with CTF events, they are information security, hacking, and malware analysis challenges where users must find hidden text, otherwise known as flags. When a flag is found, they can be submitted to the event organizer in order to receive points for your team or yourself if playing alone. The team/person with the most points at the end, wins the CTF event.

For those who have never participated in a CTF event, Facebook has stated that the challenges have been created for players of all skill levels.

The Facebook CTF is a 48 hour event running between Saturday, June 1st 2019 00:00:00 UTC through Monday, June 3rd 2019 00:00:00 UTC. At the end of the event, the team with the most points will win the following prizes:

First place: $3,133.70
Second place: $2,000.00
Third place: $1,000.00

If you want to give this event a try, you need to register an account at https://fbctf.com and jump into the challenges when they launch. The full rules and conditions can be found here.
