
	   


To get out of taking tests and doing school work, two New Jersey Secuacus High School students came up with the innovative approach of bringing down their school's WiFi signal so students could not access the online curriculum.

As much of the Secuacus High School's classwork is done via the Internet, when the two 14 year old freshmen took down the WiFi, students were unable to access their work and classes were interrupted.

Based on interviews with other students at the school, it is believed that the students were using a program or app to perform a denial of service attack on the school's WiFi equipment in order to cause it to crash or become too congested to use.

Two other cases reported last month

Last month we reported on two other cases where students hacked into their school's systems.

In Michigan, students hacked into their school's system to change their grades and and attendance records. Parents of the students who were involved were notified, but it is not known if they were charged by the police.

Also last month, a 13 year old student hacked into the school's system using a teacher's credentials. The student used this access to create a "hit-list" of students that included their names, addresess, and birth dates.
