
	   
If you get a Netflix password reset email, don't panic, as it is most likely legitimate! Recently there have been huge public postings of usernames and passwords from hacked sites such as LinkedIn and Tumblr. Though these breaches are fairly old, it wasn't until recently that the entire stockpile of information was posted publicly on the Internet.

When these types of data dumps are posted, Netflix takes the proactive approach of analyzing the decrypted passwords found within them. They then compare those passwords and associated emails with those of their subscribers, and if there is a match, automatically reset the subscriber's password. Once a password is reset, they send a notification to the subscriber explainingn why this action has taken place as shown below.


Netflix Password Reset Email for Pwned Subscribers

In my opinion, this is a very responsible way for a company to act as it not only protects their subscribers, but also protects their company from having to deal with unauthorized account logins. If you have received a message like the one above, it is strongly suggested that you follow the instructions within it.  As a security tip for dealing with any email links, instead of clicking on the links in the email, go directly to the site in your browser and follow the instructions from there.

Furthermore, if you did receive one of these emails it means that the login credentials for a hacked site was posted publicly on the Internet. If you use this same password on multiple sites, you should change those passwords to something else and try to use unique passwords on every site you visit.
