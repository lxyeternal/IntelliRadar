Contributors and those who provided new ransomware information and stories this week include: @Seifreed, @malwareforme, @malwrhunterteam, @struppigel, @BleepinComputer, @fwosar, @hexwaxwing, @jorntvdw, @FourOctets, @LawrenceAbrams, @campuscodi, @demonslay335, @PolarToffee, @DanielGallagher, @Amigo_A_, and @JAMESWT_MHT.

 

November 11th 2017

New Jigsaw Ransomware variant discovered

Michael Gillespie discovered a new variant of the Jigsaw Ransomware that appends the .##ENCRYPTED_BY_pablukl0cker## extension to encrypted files.

Lawrence Abrams discovered a new HiddenTear variant called CyberPolice. This variant appends the .locked extension to encrypted files.



New GlobeImposter variant changes how they store their configs

Michael Gillespie discovered that the newer versions of GlobeImposter changed how they encrypt their configs.

November 12th 2017

New Stroman Ransomware variant discovered

Michael Gillespie discovered a new variant of the Stroman Ransomware that is appending the .fat32 extension to encrypted files and dropping a ransom note named info.txt. Thanks to Amigo-A for identifying it.

November 13th 2017

XZZX Cryptomix Ransomware Variant Released

Lawrence Abrams discovered a new variant of the CryptoMix Ransomware being distributed that is appending the.XZZX extension to encrypted file names. 



jCandy Ransomware discovered

JAMESWT discovered a new ransomware called jCandy that appends the .locked-jCandy extension to encrypted files and drops a ransom notes named READ_ME.txt and JCANDY_INSTRUCTIONS.txt.



French Ransomware being developed

MalwareHunterTeam discovered a new in-development French ransomware that currently only encrypts C:\testrw. When encrypting files it will append the .lockon extension to encrypted files.



DrWeb able to decrypt Blind & Kill Ransomware

DrWeb has announced that they may be able to decrypt the Blind and Kill Ransomware under certain conditions and when you purchase their Rescue Pack.

New GlobeImposter variant

Lawrence Abrams discovered a new GlobeImposter Ransomware variant that drops a ransom note named how_to_back_files.html and appends the .kimchenyn extension to encrypted files.



Buggy Amnesia 2 variant discovered

Lawrence Abrams discovered a new variant of Amnesia2 Ransomware that appends the .am extension to encrypted files. Also drops a buggy ransom note. Michael Gillespie confirmed that Emsisoft's decryptor still works.



Goofed Ransomware discovered

Lawrence Abrams found a new HiddenTear variant that drops a ransom note named YOU_DONE_GOOFED.txt and appends the .goofed extension to encrypted files.

November 14th 2017

New Sexy GlobeImposter variant

Michael Gillespie found a new version of GlobeImposter uploaded to ID-Ransomware that appends the .SEXY extension to encrypted files.



November 15th 2017

Ransomware Targets J. Sterling Morton High School Students With Fake Survey

An in-development ransomware named J. Sterling Ransomware has been discovered that targets the high school students of the J. Sterling Morton school district in Cicero, Illinois by pretending to be a student survey. While this ransomware currently does not encrypt files, it shows how a developer can make an effective & targeted ransomware attack.



November 16th 2017

RASTAKHIZ  Ransomware Discovered

MalwareHunterTeam discovered a new HiddenTear variant called RASTAKHIZ that appends the .RASTAKHIZ extension to encrypted files.



 

November 17th 2017

0000 Cryptomix Ransomware Variant Released

Yesterday, MalwareHunterTeam discovered another variant of the CryptoMix ransomware, which puts it at two releases of new variants this week. This variant appends the .0000 extension to encrypted files and changes the contact emails used by the ransomware. 



WannaSmile Ransomware discovered

MalwareHunterTeam discovered a new ransomware called WannaSmile that drops a ransom note named How to decrypt files.html and appends the .WSmile extension to encrypted files.



New CorruptCrypt Ransomware discovered

MalwareHunterTeam discovered a new ransomware called CorruptCrypt that uses both the .corrupt and .acryhjccbb@protonmail.com extensions for encrypted files. This sample is still being examined and more info will be published when available.

Hand of God screenlocker discovered

Karsten Hahn found a new screenlocker called Hand of God.



BASS-FES Ransomware discovered

MalwareHunterTeam discovered a new ransomware based on HiddenTear called BASS-FES (BitchASS File Encryption System).  This ransomware appends the .basslock extension to encrypted files.


That's it for this week! Hope everyone has a nice weekend!
