
	   


A phishing scam called The HotList is the latest scam currently making its way through Instagram. This scam pretends to be a list of pictures ranked on how "Hot" they are, but just leads to a fake Instagram login page that is used to steal account logins and passwords.

This new Instagram phishing scam is based off the very successful "The Nasty List" scam that we reported about last week and operates in a similar manner.

It starts by Instagram users receiving a message from someone that says "I just saw a few of your photos on the @The_HotList_95 and they are already upvoted to #26!".

Messages being sent from hacked accounts

If a recipient goes to the listed profile, it will have a name like "Everyone Is On Here Look" and includes a description that reads "Check what position you're in!". Underneath this description will be a link to the supposed HotList. These links are typically using .me domains.

 


Example HotList Scam Profiles

If a user opens this link, they will be brought to a page that pretends to be an Instagram login page. This page looks almost identical to the real thing and contains fields where you can enter your login name and password.


Fake Instagram Login Page

While the page may look it is really a phishing scam that sends your account login information to the attackers, so you should definitely not enter your user name and password.

If you do fall victim to the HotList scam, the phishers will log into your account and use it to send further phishing messages to other Instagram users. A user who was affected by this scam told us that they while they were sitting their watching, the hackers were using their account to send a constant stream of messages to not only their followers, but also to people they didn't know.


Attacker Sending out Messages to Contacts

To avoid falling for an Instagram phishing scam like the HotList, if you are at a page that does not belong to the instagram.com web site, never enter your login credentials.

What to do if you were hacked by this scam?

If you have been hacked by the "HotList" phishing scam and you still have access to your account, the first thing you should do is verify that your account is using the correct phone number and email address.

You can do this by going to your profile and selecting Edit Profile. Then scroll to the bottom to view your email address and phone number. If it's not correct, try to change it to the correct information. 

Once you have correct email and phone number listed, you want to change your password by following these instructions.

Once you have changed your password, all devices currently logged into your account will be logged off. You can then log back in to regain control of your account.

If you do not have access to your account, you can use these instructions to report it to Instagram.
