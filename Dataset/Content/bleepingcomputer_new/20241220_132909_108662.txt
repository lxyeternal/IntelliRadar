
	   


Everyone hates robocallers right? They are annoying, intrusive, and no we don't want you to get us better search engine listings, my computer is working fine, and I don't want that free vacation where you need my birthday and social security number.

For our holiday amusement and robocaller's torture, Nomorobo and the Jolly Rogers Telephone Co have teamed up to torture unsuspecting telemarketers. Using an army chatbots that pretend to be Santa or other merry makers, telemarketers are kept on the phone as long as they can so they are unable to call other victims.

This is done using Nomorobo's robocall interception service that blocks known robocallers and the Jolly Rogers Telephone Company, who loves nothing more than unleashing their custom coded chatbots on unsuspecting telemarketers.
