
	   
In the past Google would automatically log out users G Suite users from Google web services such as Gmail or Drive after two weeks so that they would be forced to reauthenticate themselves.  As of today, Google is rolling out a new feature for Google G Suite Business, Enterprise, and Education admins that allow them to specify a custom session duration for their organization's users.

This new feature is being released after hearing from some organizations that preferred shorter or longer session timeouts depending on a particular groups activity. For example, it would be more appropriate to have a longer session timeout for desktop machines that never left the office, while for extra security, mobile users would have a much shorter session timeout.

This new feature is under the Security settings and is labeled "Google session control". From there, an admin can select a a session duration from a list of available options and apply them to a particular organization.


Source: Google

This feature is not available for those who are using G Suite for free.

For organizations currently using 2-step verification, starting Today when a new G Suite user logs into their account they will be presented with a prompt to "Remember this computer" so they do not have to perform a 2-step verification again for a certain period of time.


Source: Google

Admins now have the ability to disable the ability for users to remember their computer and instead force them to perform a 2-step verification every time they login.


Source: Google

This setting can be found under Settings->Advanced Settings and is available to all G Suite users, even those who are using it for free.
