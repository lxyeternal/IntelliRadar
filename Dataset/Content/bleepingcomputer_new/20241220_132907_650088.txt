
	   
Today Microsoft and Adobe released security updates for critical vulnerabilities in their products.  Software vulnerabilities are classified as Critical when they allow attackers to execute commands on a vulnerable computer, which could allow the attacker to also install further programs without your permission.  These vulnerabilities are the most dangerous as they are commonly utilized by exploit kits and hackers to gain full access to a computer.

In today's Patch Tuesday release, Microsoft released 13 security updates for many of their products.
