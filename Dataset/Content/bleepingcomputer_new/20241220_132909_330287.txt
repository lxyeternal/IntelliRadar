
	   


The Department of Justice announced today that a grand jury has unsealed an indictment against two Iranian hackers for conducting the hacking and ransomware operation called SamSam.

SamSam is a ransomware that has been actively targeting healthcare and government organizations since 2016 by hacking into computers and manually installing ransomware on them. Once they gained access to a computer, which was usually through hacked Remote Desktop Services, they would use that computer as a launching pad to infect and encrypt other computers on the network.

Due to the manual and targeted operation, the ransom amounts associated with each attack ranged from $5,000 USD to over $60,000 depending on the size of the attack. Over the years, this allowed the attackers to earn over $6 million dollars in ransom payments and $30 million in damages.

While most ransomware throws a wide net and hopes to catch some victims, the hackers targeted specific organizations and performed research about them before conducting their attacks.

"For example, on May 28, 2016, the defendants allegedly accessed the network of Kansas Heart Hospital and deployed the SamSam Ransomware to encrypt the hospital’s computers. They then extorted the hospital by demanding a ransom payment in Bitcoin in exchange for the decryption keys for the compromised data," stated the Department of Justice announcement. "According to the indictment, the defendants conducted online searches concerning the hospital and accessed its website a few days before the attack.  This was just one alleged example of the defendants’ efforts to select and target their victims."

According to the indictment unsealed today in the District of New Jersey, Savandi and Mansouri face the following charges:

conspiracy to commit fraud and related activity in connection with computers;
	conspiracy to commit wire fraud;
	intentional damage to a protected computer; and
	transmitting a demand in relation to damaging a protected computer.
SamSam targeted large organizations

During the press conference, Assistant Attorney General Brian A. Benczkowski, provided a list of large organizations that have been previously attacked by the SamSam operation.

This list includes the following organizations:

the City of Newark, New Jersey;
	the Colorado Department of Transportation;
	Nebraska Orthopedic Hospital;
	the City of Atlanta;
	LabCorp of America;
	Hancock Health
	MedStar Health; and
	the Port of San Diego.
