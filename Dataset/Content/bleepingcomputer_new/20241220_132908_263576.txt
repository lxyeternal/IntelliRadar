
	   
It has been a week heavily dominated by GlobeImposter variants being released here and there and smaller ransomware variants with little or no distribution. We also saw news about companies still being affected by the NotPetya attack.

Contributors and those who provided new ransomware information and stories this week include: @Seifreed, @FourOctets, @JAMESWT_MHT, @PolarToffee, @campuscodi, @LawrenceAbrams, @demonslay335, @malwareforme, @jorntvdw, @fwosar, @malwrhunterteam, @BleepinComputer, @struppigel, @DanielGallagher,@SickSkillz1, @Jan0fficial @TrendLabs, @certbund, @MarceloRivero, @siri_urz, @certbund, and @JakubKroustek.

 

July 29th 2017

GlobeImposter Variant Discovered

CERT-Bund discovered a new variant of GlobeImposter that appends the .725 extension to encrypted files.

Storm Ransomware Discovered

MalwareHunterTeam discovered the Storm Ransomware.



RansomDemoN Ransomware Discovered

MalwareHunterTeam discovered an in-development ransomware called RansomDemoN. It will only encrypt if you click on the Encrypt button.



July 30th 2017

New Samsam/Samas Variant Discovered

Michael Gillespie discovered a new variant of Samsam/Samas that appends the .supported2017 extension to encrypted files.

July 31st 2017

Crypt GlobeImposter Ransomware Distributed via Blank Slate Malspam

The "Blank Slate" malspam campaign has switched from distributing the Aleta BTCware variant to distributing a GlobeImposter variant that appends the .crypt extension. This malspam campaign is called Blank Slate due to the lack of a subject line and message body in the spam emails.



Private Builder Ransomware Discovered

Karsten Hahn discovered a builder for the Private Build Ransomware.



FCP Ransomware Discovered

Karsten Hahn discovered the FCP Ransomware, which drops a ransom note named READ_ME_HELP_ME.txt. According to S!Ri, it does not encrypt files, but simply renames them.



Oxar Ransomware Builder Discovered

Karsten Hahn discovered the builder for the Oxar Ransomware. This builder allows people to make custom versions of the ransom.



New Gryphon BTCWare Variant

A new variant of the BTCWare variant was discovered by Karsten Hahn that appends the [decr@cock.li].gryphon extension to encrypted files.



Dancing ScreenLocker Discovered

MalwareHunterTeam discovered a Polish screenlocker with an animated background. Does not encrypt.



Another variant of the Test Ransomware Discovered

SickSkillz discovered another variant of the Test Ransomware.



New GlobeImposter variant wants to give you a rose

Jakub Kroustek discovered a new GlobeImposter that appends the .rose extension to encrypted files.



Another GlobeImposter variant released

Marcelo Rivero discovered another GlobeImposter variant that appends the .ocean extension and drops a note called !back_files!.html.



New BTCWare Variant Appends the .Blocking Extension

Marcelo Rivero discovered a new BTCWare variant that appends the avalona.toga@aol.com].blocking extension and drops a ransom note named !#_READ_ME_#!.hta.

Scotch Tape Screenlocker

MalwareHunterTeam discovered a new screenlocker called Scotch Tape Locker v1.0.



 

August 1st 2017

US Pharma Giant "Not yet Producing Bulk Product" Because of NotPetya Outbreak

US pharmaceutics giant Merck was severely affected by the NotPetya ransomware outbreak that took place at the end of June, and the company is still struggling to restore all systems and resume normal operations, according to an 8-K report filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

RSA2048Pro Ransomware

MalwareHunterTeam discovered the RSA2048Pro  ransomware.

SevenDays Ransomware Discovered

Lawrence Abrams discovered the SevenDays Ransomware. This ransomware appends .SEVENDAYS to encrypted file names but does not leave any payment instructions. May be a joke ransomware.



TPS1.0 Ransomware/Screenlocker Discovered

Lawrence Abrams discovered the in-dev TPS1.0 Ransomware/Screenlocker. It does not encrypt and only shows the form.



GlobeImposter Adds the .726 Extension

Marcelo Rivero found a new variant of the GlobeImposter that adds the .726 extension to encrypted files.

Ranaomware Ransomware Discovered

Marcelo Rivero discovered the Ranaomware Ransomware that renames files but does not add an extension. Drops a ransom note named README_1910092_#####.txt.



August 2nd 2017

LockBox Ransomware Discovered

Karsten Hahn discovered the LockBox ransomware. 



Crystal Ransomware Discovered

Karsten Hahn discovered the Crystal malware, which includes a downloader, flooder, and ransomware. It does not appear to append an extension to encrypted file names.

RobinHood Ransomware

S!Ri discovered the RobinHood ransomware, which is a political statement against Saudi Arabia.



WannaPay Ransomware Discovered

Karsten Hahn discovered the in-development WannaPay ransomware. Current uses the C:\Users\DORA hardcoded path for encryption and ransom note testing.

EbayWall is a Ransomware with a Story

With most ransomware being the same as every other one, its interesting to find one that is a bit different. This is the case with EbayWall discovered by Jakub Kroustek, whose ransom note tells a strange little story. This ransomware appends the .ebay extension and drops a ransom note named ebay-msg.html.



August 3rd 2017

Ukrainian Firm Facing Legal Action for Damages Caused by NotPetya Ransomware

The Juscutum Attorneys Association, a Ukrainian law firm, is rallying NotPetya victims to join a collective lawsuit against Intellect-Service LLC, the company behind the M.E.Doc accounting software, the point of origin of the NotPetya ransomware outbreak.

GlobeImposter's Keep Coming

MalwareHunterTeam discoverd a new GlobeImposter variant that appends the .sea extension to encrypted files and drops a ransom note named !your_files!.html .

August 4th 2017

Cerber Ransomware Can Now Steal Browser Passwords, Bitcoin Wallet Data

According to Gilbert Sison and Janus Agcaoili, two security researchers at Trend Micro, the most recent version of the Cerber ransomware can dump browser passwords and can steal files related to Bitcoin wallets.

Shutdown57 Ransomware Discovered

Michael Gillespie discovered the Shutdown57 Ransomware. This ransomware appends the .shutdown57 extension and drops a ransom note named shutdown57.php.



New GlobeImposter Variant Appends .490

Jakub Kroustek discovered a new GlobeImposter variant that appends the .490 extension to encrypted files and drops the free_files!.html ransom note. According to Jakub, this may be a test version.



Oxar Ransomware Gets a Facelift

Jakub Kroustek noticed that a new release of the Oxar Ransomware had a redesign. Still decryptable.



That's it for this week! Hope everyone has a nice weekend!
