
	   


Emsisoft has released a decryptor for the MegaLocker and NamPoHyu Virus ransomware that has been targeting exposed Samba servers. Victims can now use this decryptor to recover their files for free.

Last month we reported that the MegaLocker NamPoHyu Virus Ransomware infections are targeting exposed Samba servers and encrypting their data remotely. At that time we stated that users should not pay the ransomware as a method to decrypt the files may have been discovered.

We are happy to announce that a decryptor is now available from Emsisoft that can decrypt victims of the MegaLocker and NamPoHyu Virus for free.

Ransomware dev not happy

When we told victims to not pay for the ransomware, the developer was obviously not happy. 

In a series of posts to our MegaLocker topic in the forums, the ransomware developer threatened to double the ransom amounts if a decryptor was not released within a week.


Post by NamPoHyu Developer

The developer became increasingly frustrated and went as far as to call our helpers "devils". They then threatened to change the encryption algorithm used by the ransomware.


Another post by ransomware developer
