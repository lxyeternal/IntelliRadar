
	   
This week was definitely a lot busier than the previous one. During the past two weeks we have had some interesting ransomware released such as Zenis, which deletes files associated with backups, and AVCrypt that tries to uninstall your security software. We also had a bunch of organizations, including local governments, affected by ransomware infections, which were most likely the SamSam variants.

Ransomware may seem like it's dying down, but as the stories continue to come out about large organizations being infected, it is still incredibly important to make sure you have good backups and to not open unknown attachments. Be smart and stay safe!

March 11th 2018

Crypt888: being lazy doesn’t pay

When looking at some of the „old hands“ of the ransomware scene, you will quickly realize that a lot of development work and quality assurance has gone into them. Those often very elaborate pieces of malware deliberately make the life of an analyst more difficult by either leaving fals trails or leading analysts down one dead end after another. In the case of “Crypt888” you might argue that it is not even a ransomware in the classical sense of the term, as no money is demanded for decrypting the data.

Necurs and Gamut Botnets Account for 97% of the Internet's Spam Emails

Just two botnets accounted for 97% of all spam emails in the last three months of 2017, according to a McAfee report released earlier today.

Sigma Ransomware Being Distributed Using Fake Craigslist Malspam

Today one of our volunteers, Aura, told me about a new new malspam campaign pretending to be from Craigslist that is under way and distributing the Sigma Ransomware. These spam emails contain password protected Word or RTF documents that download the Sigma Ransomware executable from a remote site and install it on a recipients computer.



March 13th 2018

Paradise Ransomware is still active

Michael Gillespie found a new sample of currently active Paradise Ransomware campaign. This version has the extension [id-].[support@all-ransomware.info].sell and drops a ransom note named #DECRYPT MY FILES# .html.



New VBRansom Ransomware

Leo discovered a new in-dev ransomware called VBRansom that currently does not encrypt.



New L0cked variant

Leo spotted a new L0cked variant.



March 14th 2018

Korean Jigsaw variant

Michael Gillespie found a new Jigsaw Ransomware variant that uses the .email-[powerhacker03@hotmail.com].koreaGame extension.

Hermes ransomware distributed to South Koreans via recent Flash zero-day

Malwarebytes has a good blog post about a recent Flash zero-day is being used to distribute the Hermes Ransomware.



March 15th 2018

Geographical Region With the Most Ransomware Encounters in 2017 Was Asia

According to Microsoft, based on analytics the company recorded between February and December 2017, Asia was the geographical region that recorded the greatest number of ransomware encounters last year.



US Sanctions Russia Over NotPetya Outbreak, Energy Grid Hacks, Election Meddling

The United States has imposed sanctions against Russian entities for the NotPetya ransomware outbreak, cyber-attacks on the US power grid, and their attempts to influence the 2016 US presidential election process.

March 16th 2018

Zenis Ransomware Encrypts Your Data & Deletes Your Backups

A new ransomware was discovered by MalwareHunterTeam this week called Zenis Ransomware. While it is currently unknown how Zenis is being distributed, multiple victims have already become infected with this ransomware. What is most disturbing about Zenis is that it not encrypts your files, but also purposely deletes your backups.



Weekly Threat Report 16th March 2018

In the NCSC UK weekly threat report, we learn that the Colorado Department of Transportation IT system was infected by SamSam.

We previously reported on SamSam (or Samas) ransomware infecting Colorado Department of Transportation (CDOT) computers. Although the organisation had begun to recover from this cyber incident, international media state that CDOT has been affected by another ransomware infection only a week after the original attack. Reports suggest that computers have been re-infected with a new variant of the SamSam ransomware. 

March 17th 2018

Author of Polski, Vortex, and Flotera Ransomware Families Arrested in Poland

Polish law enforcement announced on Friday the arrest of Tomasz T., a well-known cyber-criminal believed to be the author of the Polski, Vortex, and Flotera ransomware strains.

March 19th 2018

VB2017 paper: The life story of an IPT - Inept Persistent Threat actor

This is an interesting video to watch regarding the Vortex Ransomware actor via Virus Bulletin.


Last Wednesday, Belgium-based Polish hacker Thomasz T. was arrested during a visit to his home country. Thomasz is believed to be the author of the Polski, Vortex, and Flotera ransomware families.

Though this may look impressive on one's cybercrime CV, it turns out that Thomasz may not be the most skilled of hackers. At VB2017 in Madrid, Polish security researcher and journalist Adam Haertlé presented a paper about him, the title of which is quite telling: "The life story of an IPT - Inept Persistent Threat actor".


Stinger Ransomware Discovered

Jakub Kroustek discovered the Stinger Ransomware. Uses a ransom note named About .Stinger unlocking instructions.txt and the .Stinger extension.



March 20th 2018

Finger Lakes Health reports security break

The Finger Lakes Health medical center was infected with a ransomware infection. It is unknown what they were infected with.

March 21st 2018

New R2D2 Technique Protects Files Against Wiper Malware

Purdue University scientists have developed a data protection technique called Reactive Redundancy for Data Destruction (R2D2) that can protect data sitting inside a virtual machine from modern data-wiping malware and even some secure file deletion methods.

March 22nd 2018

City of Atlanta IT Systems Hit by SamSam Ransomware

The Mayor of Atlanta, Georgia has confirmed today in a press conference that several local government systems are currently down due to a ransomware infection.

TrickBot Banking Trojan Gets Screenlocker Component

According to MalwareTech, the most recent version of the TrickBot banking trojan now includes a screenlocker component, suggesting the malware's operators might soon start holding victims for ransom if infected targets don't appear to be e-banking users.



New L0cked variant

Karsten Hahn found a new L0cked variant that has an extension of %s%s%s.lckd.

March 23rd 2018

The AVCrypt Ransomware Tries To Uninstall Your AV Software

MalwareHunterTeam discovered a new ransomware named AVCrypt that tries to uninstall existing security software before it encrypts a computer.  Furthermore, as it removes numerous services, including Windows Update, and provides no contact information, this ransomware may be a wiper.



Rapid 2.0 Ransomware Released, Will Not Encrypt Data on PCs with Russian Locale

MalwareHunterTeam discovered Rapid 2.0 ransomware, which as the name hints, is v2.0 of the Rapid ransomware.
