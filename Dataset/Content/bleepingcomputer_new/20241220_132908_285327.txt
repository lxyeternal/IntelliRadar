
	   
Over the past two weeks, there has been some interesting ransomware news regarding a new GandCrab decryptor and two new ransomware infections.

First, BitDefender released a decryptor for GandCrab versions 5.1 and lower.  This will allow victims of these versions to get their files back for free.

We then had the B0r0nt0K Ransomware, which appears to target Linux servers using PHP, but could also target Windows machines who have PHP installed. The second ransomware is Cr1ptT0r, which is targeting D-Link routers that are no longer supported.

Contributors and those who provided new ransomware information and stories this week include: @PolarToffee, @jorntvdw, @struppigel, @demonslay335, @malwareforme, @FourOctets, @BleepinComputer, @Seifreed, @fwosar, @LawrenceAbrams, @malwrhunterteam, @Emm_ADC_Soft, @jeromesegura, @BitdefenderLabs, @symantec, @TDOnline, @GrujaRS, @Dlink, and @shotgunner101.
