In an email to Bleeping Computer, an NCA spokesperson confirmed the arrests and provided the following statement.

We can confirm that NCA officers executed a search warrant at an address in Natal Road, London SW16 on the evening of Thursday 19 January. A man and a woman were arrested and later bailed until April 2017. Enquiries are on-going and we are unable to provide further information at this time.

Two 50-year-olds behind the attack

British media first reported the arrests yesterday, saying the two suspects are both 50-years-old, a British man, and a Swedish woman.

The two are the main suspects behind a cyber-attack that hit the US capital a week before President Trump's inauguration.

Investigators believe the suspects had broken into the CCTV network of Washington, DC, and left ransomware behind that affected 123 of 187 video recording systems, around 65% of all of the US capital's camera surveillance system.

Ransomware attack hit Washington ahead of Trump's big day

The CCTV hijacks took place between January 12 and 15, sending city officials into panic mode, fearing more powerful attacks for President Trump's official inauguration ceremony, which was held on January 20.

City officials didn't pay the ransom demand, and IT staff took care of the ransomware incident by wiping systems and reinstalling software on all affected machines. The CCTV systems were back and fully functional in time for President Trump's inauguration ceremony.

Due to the sensitive nature of the attack, several agencies were called in to investigate, such as the Secret Service and the FBI. Investigators said they found two different ransomware strains on the compromised systems.
