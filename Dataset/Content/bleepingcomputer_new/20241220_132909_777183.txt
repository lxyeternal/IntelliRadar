Started in May 2020 by two well-known reverse engineers, Vitali Kremez and Daniel Bunce (aka 0verfl0w_), Zero2Automated is more than just an online training course.

Instead, participants participate in an online community on Discord, where students can chat with their peers and teachers as they work through 25+ hours of malware analysis and reverse engineering content.
