While many of VirusTotal's features are free, they also have a paid VirusTotal Intelligence service that is primarily used by security companies and malware researchers to gain access to new malware samples, perform searches for malware that contain certain characteristics, and to setup "Hunting" rules that notify users when matching malware is submitted to VirusTotal.

With VirusTotal Enterprise, Chronicle is now targeting the enterprise with premium features that can help organizations discover and mitigate new threats and compromises. These new features include expanded Yara threat hunting, faster searching and accuracy, more detailed information about submitted files, and the ability for organizations to find other malware that share the same characteristics of samples they may be infected with.

Premium VirusTotal Graph features

VirusTotal Graph is a free service that allows you to research a file submitted to VirusTotal by visualizing interconnected nodes that represent different data points. These data points include what network requests a file makes, what files it drops, what sites may be hosting the file, and more. You can also double-click on individual nodes to dig down deeper into that specific data point.

Using this information, administrators and researchers can investigate how a malware sample is spread, what actions it performs on a computer, and any relationships it may have with another infection.

While VirusTotal Graph is a free service for all registered users, there are some extra features that are available to premium customers. These premium features include the ability to create private graphs that are only visible to your organization or those you share it with and the ability to add your own data points to indicate a bad actor, an organization's department, email address, or victims.


VirusTotal Enterprise Graph

By adding custom data nodes, organizations can visualize how a malware infection spreads in their network or find other related information that indicates a further compromise, threats that need to be mitigated, or networks and domains that should be blacklisted.

Private graphs also offer the ability to extract commonalities from nodes to generate indicators of compromise. For example, in the graph below you can see all of the URLs and network requests related to the selected file.


Malware Sample Relationships

With private graphs, organizations can choose to use them internally or share it with third-party researchers. In the event of a compromise, this allows a company to share the graph with a consultant or security firm who may be assisting them in mitigating an attack.
