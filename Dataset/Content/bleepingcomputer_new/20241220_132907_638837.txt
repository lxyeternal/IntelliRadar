Redmond provides Microsoft 365 Business subscription service customers with all the productivity features in the Office 365 suite coupled with security capabilities and advanced device management to help them run their organizations in the cloud.

Conditional Access features are now available to all Microsoft 365 Business subscribers and they allow customers to configure granular Conditional Access Policies from the Azure Portal with the help of the Azure Active Directory Settings.

How Conditional Access works

"By configuring Conditional Access policies you can maintain control over how and where your company data is accessed, making your business more secure. You can define exact criteria for who can gain access and block those who don't meet the criteria," says Microsoft.

The new Conditional Access support added to Microsoft 365 Business enables SMBs to enable Zero Trust security to protect the organization's assets while still being in control of who can connect to the Office 365 environment, allowing employees to access it remotely if they meet the requirements.

As Microsoft further explains, "you can define a Conditional Access policy that evaluates sign-in connections from mobile devices to Exchange Online, and requires employees use Outlook for iOS and Android to successfully access their work email and calendar."


Adding Conditional Access policies

Back in early-January, Microsoft announced the addition of two new compliance and security offerings in response to the new requirements added by information protection regulations such as EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as well as the increasing cybersecurity attack threat.

Later that month, Microsoft expanded Microsoft 365's privacy and personal data protection features to make it possible for customers to fully respect the provisions of privacy laws enacted on a worldwide basis after EU's GDPR went into effect.

Microsoft 365 Business also got upgraded with additional on-premises password writeback support during January, allowing IT administrators to provide users on their domain with the means to unlock their accounts or change their passwords using Self Service Password Reset in the cloud and have it "written back to an existing on-premises directory in real time."
