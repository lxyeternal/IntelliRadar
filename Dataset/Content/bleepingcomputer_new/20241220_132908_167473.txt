A post from the owner of SocialEngineered.net announced on Thursday that the forum had been breached via a vulnerability in the MyBB forum software.

The last major MyBB vulnerability was disclosed publicly on June 11 along with the technical details. Successful exploitation of the bug - a stored cross-site scripting (XSS) and file write, leads to remote code execution and complete takeover of the target site.

Data shared on cracking forums

The data from SocialEngineered.net was dumped on a hacker forum on June 13. The individual leaking the information posted that he had "uploaded the full database and root directory of this website."

The dump affects 55,121 users of the social engineering forum and includes their usernames, passwords stored as salted MD5 hashes, email addresses, IP addresses, and private messages.



It appears that the hacker was able to get much more than this, though, as the post on the rival forum informed that the leak also contained the website source code, data, and activity.

HaveIBeenPwned breach notification platform added the new database to its collection and informed on Sunday that it included 89,000 unique email addresses from 55,000 forum users, thus confirming the initial details.

On Friday, the same database was uploaded to another hacker forum where multiple members shared positive reactions at its availability.



Following the hack, SocialEngineered moved to the XenForo forum platform to avoid a similar incident in the future. The owner called for immediately changing of the login passwords from members' part.

As details on new vulnerabilities appear, hackers are quick to take advantage of them and start looking for potential targets. This incident is a clear example of how fast attackers can strike, as the threat actor needed just two days to compromise a website and publish the details obtained this way.
