Flipper Zero is a portable multi-function tool allowing pen-testers and hacking enthusiasts to tinker with a wide range of hardware using RFID emulation, digital access key cloning, radio communications, NFC, infrared, Bluetooth, and more.

Although it has sparked controversy after videos demonstrated it being used to open garage doors, unlock cars, clone RFID chips, and trigger doorbells, it remains a powerful educational and security research tool for those using it responsibly.

Previously, Flipper Zero users had to source apps for the device on their own, typically from code repositories like GitHub, and load them onto the device via a microSD card. "With the launch of Flipper Apps, users can extend their devices' functionality with custom applications."

Flipper's announcement for the launch of Flipper Apps highlights the following apps:

Flipper Authenticator: Implements two-step verification for major services via TOTP.
	Flipper Air Mouse: Turns Flipper into a USB or Bluetooth air mouse to control devices.
	Games: Tetris, Minesweeper, Arkanoid, Asteroids, Flappy Bird, poker, and other games adapted for Flipper Zero's screen, plus radio module multiplayer options.
	Temp sensor readers: App for reading temperature, humidity, and pressure sensors like DHT11/22, DS18B20, etc.
	Xbox Controller: An IR remote control for Xbox One.
	Multi Fuzzer: Pen-testing app for vulnerability checks on iButton/RFID readers.
	Seader: Reads credentials from various card formats (requires adapters) and saves for later use.
Even though Flipper Zero has faced challenges in certain national markets, such as Brazil, and on online platforms like Amazon, where it's categorized as a card skimming tool, its popularity as a portable penetration testing device remains unmatched.

The introduction of Flipper Apps is poised to expand its user base, drawing in more mainstream audiences and further solidifying its market presence and success.
