
	   


Today Adobe issued Security Bulletin APSB16-01, which is an Adobe Flash update that fixes 19 reported vulnerabilities. These vulnerabilities are all critical as they could potentially allow an attacker to take control of an affected system.  Adobe is also aware of a report that an exploit for CVE-2015-8651 is being used in limited, targeted attacks.  

The CVE identifiers fixed by this update are CVE-2015-8459, CVE-2015-8460, CVE-2015-8634, CVE-2015-8635, CVE-2015-8636, CVE-2015-8638, CVE-2015-8639, CVE-2015-8640, CVE-2015-8641, CVE-2015-8642, CVE-2015-8643, CVE-2015-8644, CVE-2015-8645, CVE-2015-8646, CVE-2015-8647, CVE-2015-8648, CVE-2015-8649, CVE-2015-8650, CVE-2015-8651.
