
	   
It has been mostly small variants released this week, with a few Scarab variants released and various U.S. government agencies being hit with ransomware. Otherwise, it's mostly ransomware that will not make it into the actual wild.

While the news stories that we are reporting on are mostly new ransomware, ransomware such as GandCrab and Samas continue to make their presence known. All users need to stay vigilant and secure access to remote desktop so they do not become infected.

May 12th 2018

Police Dept Loses 10 Months of Work to Ransomware. Gets Infected a Second Time!

Ransomware has infected the servers of the Riverside Fire and Police department for the second time in a month. The first ransomware infection took place on April 23, last month and encrypted ten months worth of work data related to active investigations.

S!Ri discovered a ransomware called CryptON that is based off the Nemesis Ransomware.  This ransomware is VM aware and appends the .#########.ransomed@india.com extension, where ######### is the user id. The ransomware also drops a ransom note named HOWTODECRYPTFILES.html.



May 13th 2018

New RSAUtil Ransomware variant

Amigo-A found a new RSAUtil ransomware variant that uses the emails Tizer78224@gmx.de, Tizer78224@india.com, and Tizer77234@protonmail.com.



 

May 14th 2018

StalinLocker Deletes Your Files Unless You Enter the Right Code

A new in-development screenlocker/wiper called StalinLocker, or StalinScreamer, was discovered by MalwareHunterTeam that gives you 10 minutes to enter a code or it will try to delete the contents of the drives on the computer. While running, it will display screen that shows Stalin while playing the USSR anthem and displaying a countdown until files are deleted.



Rapid Ransomware 3 in the wild

MalwareHunterTeam noticed that the Rapid Ransomware is in the wild and that victims have been detected in multiple counties. This ransomware also pages homage in an unwanted way to Michael Gillespie through the use of the contact email address.



Ransomware attack hits 8000 clients of Family Planning NSW

Financial Review reported that:

A ransomware attack on Family Planning NSW has potentially exposed the personal information of 8000 clients who used the service in the last 2.5 years, with the organisation revealing the breach to its users under the new notifiable data breach scheme.

Sepsis Ransomware discovered

MalwareHunterTeam discovered a new ransomware called Sepsis ransomware that uses the extension .[Sepsis@protonmail.com].SEPSIS and drops a ransom note named Info.hta.



May 15th 2018

New Bip Dharma Ransomware Variant Released

Today, Michael Gillespie noticed what appeared to be a new variant of the Crysis/Dharma Ransomware uploaded to his ID-Ransomware site. Jakub Kroustek then discovered some samples to confirm that it was indeed a new Dharma variant. This new version will append the .Bip extension to encrypted files. It is not known exactly how this variant is being distributed, but in the past Dharma is typically spread by hacking into Remote Desktop Services and manually installing the ransomware.



New Walker Scarab Ransomware variant

Amigo-A discovered a new variant of the Scarab Ransomware called Walker that drops a ransom note named HOW TO DECRYPT WALKER INFO.TXT and base64 encodes a encrypted file's name and append the .JohnnieWalker extension.



May 16th 2018

Horsuke Scarab Ransomware discovered

S!Ri discovered a new variant of the Scarab Ransomware called Horsuke that uses the horsuke@nuke.africa email address.



New Jigsaw Ransomware variant discovered

Michael Gillespie discovered a new Jigsaw variant that uses different text and background. When encrypting files it will append the .booknish extension to encrypted files.



May 17th 2018

Sigrun Ransomware spotted

Michael Gillespie discovered a ransomware called Sigrun uploaded to ID-Ransomware that utilizes the .sigrun extension and a ransom note named RESTORE-SIGRUN.txt.



Mr. Dec Ransomware discovered

Michael Gillespie discovered a ransomware called Mr. Dec uploaded to ID-Ransomware that utilizes the .[ID][ID] extension and a ransom note named Decoding help.hta.



That's it for this week! Hope everyone has a nice weekend!
