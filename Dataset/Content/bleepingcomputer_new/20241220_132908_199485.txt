
	   
Sophos has announced yesterday the purchase of Netherlands based security software company SurfRight for $31.8 million.  HitmanPro, which we have been a big fan of since it was released, has developed innovative and powerful technology that detects zero-day exploits, remediates advanced persistent threat (APT) vectors, does a great job removing malware, and protects your computer from file encrypting ransomware. This purchase allows Sophos to integrate this technology into their products in order to further strengthen their endpoint protection features.
