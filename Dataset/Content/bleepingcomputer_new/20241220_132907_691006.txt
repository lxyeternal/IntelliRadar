
	   


Microsoft released the December 2019 non-security Microsoft Office updates that fix issues and add stability and performance improvements to Windows Installer (MSI) editions of Office 2016.

For example, this month's updates fix an authentication issue when connecting Microsoft Project to Project Server online that throws an error saying that "The server you are trying to access is using an authentication protocol not supported by this version of Office." 

After installing the Office 2016 KB4484170 update, you will be able to sign in again successfully.

Two of the Office December non-security updates only apply to Outlook and Skype for Business 2016, while the third addresses issues present in Standard, Professional Plus, Professional, Home and Student, as well as Home and Business editions of Office 2016.

All the updates issued today by Microsoft can be downloaded from the Download Center or the Microsoft Update service for manual installation.

None of the Microsoft Office updates released today apply to the Click-to-Run Office apps such as Microsoft Office 365 Home.

The list of updates issued today and the Office product they apply to is available below.

Product
			Knowledge Base article
		Microsoft Office 2016
			KB4484170
		Microsoft Outlook 2016
			KB4484172
		Skype for Business 2016
			KB4484133
		Some updates may require a restart

Before updating, it is important to mention that after applying these updates you may also be required to restart your computer.

If your Office installation starts misbehaving after updating, you can uninstall the offending update using the following step by step procedure:


Go to Start, enter View Installed Updates in the Search Windows box, and then press Enter.
	In the list of updates, locate and select the offending update, and then select Uninstall.


Depending on the update you want to install, you may also have to install other updates for the issue to be completely fixed on your Windows device.

This is the case of the KB4484133 update for Skype for Business 2016 that requires KB4011669 to also be installed, along with KB3203472 or KB4011099
