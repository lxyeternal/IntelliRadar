
	   


As the eagerly anticipated tactical FPS game Valorant ends their closed beta, a fake mobile version is being distributed that displays nothing but scams to those who install it.

Riot Game's Valorant ended closed beta today as they prepare for their official launch on June 2nd, 2020.

Initially being launched for the PC, Valorant is not available for mobile devices as of yet, though they have stated they are looking into it for the future.

Fake Valorant mobile apps promoted on YouTube

These video descriptions contain links to a web site that promote download links for Valorant Android & iOS,

If a user tries to download the iOS version, they will be brought to an affiliate site offering various offers that a user must complete to download the mobile app.


iOS affiliate offers

Completing these offers only generate revenue for the scammers and do not provide an actual Valorant mobile app.

Android users, on the other hand, will be prompted to download and install a 'Valorant.apk' as illustrated by Dr. Web below.


Android download experience

Once installed and started, the app will first display a convincing Valorant screen, as shown below.


Fake Valorant for Android app

Before you can try the game, though, the app will state that you need to download "two free apps to unlock game!"


Prompt to unlock the game

Clicking on the OK button will open the same affiliate offer site shown to iOS, where they will be prompted to complete two offers.

While affiliate offers can generate real value, such as in-game currency for legitimate apps, these offers do not unlock the Valorant mobile app because it does not exist.

"In fact, some of these services provide real rewards to users. For example, they can receive an in-game currency or other game bonuses upon completing the task. However, in the case of the Android.FakeApp.176 trojan, users receive no reward at all. The mobile version of the Valorant game does not currently exist, and the only purpose of this fake app is to load the affiliate website to help malware developers receive rewards by taking advantage of their victims," explain Dr. Web in their report.

This particular scam just displays affiliate offers, but there is nothing to stop a threat actor from also installing malware on an Android device.

If you have installed any Valorant mobile apps, they are fake and should be uninstalled immediately.
