
Every year at Checkmarx, we recognize and award our business partners who have gone above and beyond the call of duty, helping their customers improve the security of the software they develop. These awards reflect our deep appreciation of the vital role our partners play in Checkmarx’s success, and more importantly, the enormous value they create for organizations who adopt Checkmarx AppSec solutions.
This past year has been like no other and it certainly demonstrated just how creative, determined, and committed our partners are. They have supported Checkmarx customers who are undergoing significant pressure to digitally transform their business at an unprecedented rate. Organizations agree that software development is imperative to continual business growth, and the security of that software is paramount.
With EMEA and APAC being 100% channel-focused regions for Checkmarx, we count on our partners to not only help us scale and grow, but also to be our eyes and ears on the ground. Our partners help us support our customers by identifying the unique challenges they face in the different regions and unique business environments. They also champion the DevSecOps community and deliver vital technical support in local territories and languages. So, whatever the issue, there is a local Checkmarx-trained professional available to help.
During this year’s awards event, we held a ceremony to recognize the support and close collaboration achieved by leading Checkmarx partners across EMEA and APAC. This year’s winners were selected from more than 250 partners based upon their collaboration with Checkmarx and our customers, the high standards of technology and support they offer, their overall commitment to increase security awareness, and provide solutions that directly address secure software development and digital transformation.
All our partners have reached very high achievements in this most-unusual year, and this year’s winners have exceeded all expectations. Congratulations to all the winners listed below—very well deserved!
