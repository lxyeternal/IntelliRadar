PyPi on Hold: Suspends New Users' and Projects Creations Due to A High Volume of Malicious Activity | by Tzachi(Zack) Zorn | checkmarx-security | Medium PyPi on Hold: Suspends New Users' and Projects Creations Due to A High Volume of Malicious Activity Tzachi(Zack) Zorn · Follow Published in checkmarx-security · 3 min read · May 20 -- 1 Listen Share A few hours ago, the PyPi team announced that they are temporarily suspending the creation of new users and the publication of new projects after detecting a high volume of malicious activity that they are unable to respond to in a timely fashion. Large-Scale Attack Trend In the past few months, we have witnessed actors publishing overwhelming amounts of malicious packages in several open-source registries; among these is the NPM incident early last month that flooded the JavaScript package manager and caused a sporadic denial of service. Late last year, we saw a similar incident on the Nuget package manager, which included over 140,000 malicious packages. Just last week, we saw a spike in package publication on PyPI. Package publication spike Example: OaxStealer Just today we saw an actor using 3 different user accounts to publish dozens of malicious packages designed to steal users’ data. Hence, we must change the mindset from detecting attacks to identifying attackers. Only by understanding the attackers' TTPs and raising the bar will we succeed in keeping the open-source ecosystem safe. This will require the open-source ecosystem to develop new infrastructure and invest more in sharing attack data. We are grateful for the work done by the PyPi team and believe the industry should better support their efforts which enable the open-source ecosystem to thrive. Developers and organizations looking to invest in the right frameworks, we encourage you to look at OpenSSF S2C2F and community projects such as the Overlay extension .
