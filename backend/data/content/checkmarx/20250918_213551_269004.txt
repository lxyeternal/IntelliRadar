Award honors the best DevSecOps solution enabling security to be included earlier and continuously throughout the SDLC
PARAMUS, N.J. – January 22, 2025 – Checkmarx, the industry leader in cloud-native application security for the enterprise, has announced that its market-leading Checkmarx One application security (AppSec) platform has won Best DevSecOps Solution in the Tools and Services category of the 2024 DevOps Dozen Awards by the Techstrong Group.
“We’re proud to see this validation of our vision and accomplishments in fortifying Checkmarx One to become the AppSec solution of choice for the cloud-native enterprise,” said Checkmarx VP of Product Ori Bendet. “The need for a robust, comprehensive and consolidated platform that can seamlessly integrate anywhere withing the workflow of time-strapped developers is growing rapidly. The Checkmarx commitment is to continue to meet current needs and to anticipate the future needs of development and security teams as the threat landscape evolves.”
Checkmarx One delivers the most complete suite of enterprise AppSec solutions available within a unified, cloud-based platform that allows organizations to secure their applications from the first line of code to deployment in the cloud. It offers everything that enterprise security and development teams need to integrate AppSec across every stage of the software development life cycle (SDLC) and build a successful AppSec program while keeping developers working in their preferred integrated development environments (IDEs) and DevOps workflows.
The Techstrong Group wrote, “This award honors the best DevSecOps solution, which enables security to be included earlier and continuously throughout the software development life cycle (SDLC).” Techstrong Group founder and CEO Alan Shimel said, “For the cloud development generation, Checkmarx One is designed to help DevSecOps teams secure their applications from the first line of code to deployment in the cloud.”
The Best DevSecOps Solution award follows on the heels of two recent honors for Checkmarx One. In December the company was positioned as a Leader and Fast Mover in the inaugural GigaOm Radar Report for Software Supply Chain Security for 2024. That same month Frost and Sullivan named Checkmarx the Competitive Strategy Leader in the global application security posture management (ASPM) industry. For more information, visit this page.
