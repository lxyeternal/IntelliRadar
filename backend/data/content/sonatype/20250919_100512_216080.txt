Award Categories: Recognizing Excellence Across the SDLC
To help spotlight the different ways teams raise the bar, the Elevate Awards span six categories. Each category reflects not only an achievement, but also a philosophy for how modern organizations can succeed in today's rapidly evolving software industry.
Leader of the Year
This category recognizes outstanding industry leaders across the software development life cycle (SDLC).
Finalists in this area demonstrate clear ROI for their organizations — whether in saved time, dollars, or improved security posture. They have seamlessly integrated Sonatype solutions into their existing systems and cultivated a culture where innovation and robust security practices coexist.
These leaders show how software developers can thrive in environments where productivity and morale are strengthened by strong leadership.
DevSecOps Trailblazing Team of the Year
Being a trailblazer in DevSecOps requires more than just adopting tools. It requires establishing best practices that elevate the industry.
Finalists in this category leverage advanced technologies, such as AI/ML, SBOM management at scale, and Sonatype solutions to improve efficiencies and security. They've positioned their organizations at the forefront of DevSecOps, proving how innovation and thought leadership can transform not only internal teams, but also the wider ecosystem.
Fastest Time to Value
Adopting new solutions often comes with a learning curve, but finalists in this category have shown extraordinary results within their first year of using Sonatype.
These organizations clearly defined their purchase goals, prioritized effectively, and achieved measurable improvements faster than expected. Whether through accelerated integration, rapid ROI, or immediate developer benefits, their success stories demonstrate the impact of moving quickly without sacrificing quality.
Tech Debt Destroyer
Technical debt is one of the most common roadblocks to innovation, but the finalists here refused to let it stand in their way. By tackling legacy challenges, prioritizing improvements, and systematically reducing debt, these organizations have unlocked new capacity for innovation.
Developers at these companies now work in environments where productivity, morale, and software quality are significantly enhanced — proof that cleaning up the past is essential to building the future.
Software Supply Chain Impact
This category celebrates organizations that have taken a comprehensive approach to improving security and performance across the enterprise.
By empowering DevOps, Development, and AppSec teams to work more effectively together, finalists here are building faster, more secure software at scale. Their efforts show the value of "shift left and shift right" strategies, tool consolidation, and full supply chain visibility.
Collaboration Catalyst
The best software is built when silos are broken down. Finalists in this category have made collaboration central to their transformation journeys.
By fostering communication between security, development, and operations teams, they've improved OSS risk reduction and developer productivity, while ensuring Sonatype solutions deliver enterprise-wide impact. Their stories demonstrate that collaboration is not just a cultural goal, but a business advantage.
Looking Ahead
The 2025 Elevate Awards finalists remind us what's possible when organizations commit to both innovation and security. Their stories illustrate how technical excellence, leadership, and collaboration can reshape industries and inspire others.
The winners will be announced in October 2025, and we can't wait to celebrate their achievements.
Until then, we invite you to join us to congratulate this year's outstanding finalists and recognize their contributions to a stronger, more secure software future.
